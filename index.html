Welcome

Thank you for choosing INNOVATEX.

Your INNOVATEX system helps you keep an eye on your property and understand what is happening around it. You can check the live camera, see sensor information, receive warnings, and control supported lights or alarms from the dashboard.

In simple words: the system watches, reports, and alerts you when something needs your attention.
🛡️

Security

Check movement and possible activity around your property.

🌡️

Environment

See temperature, humidity, light, and distance information.

📱

Easy Access

Use the dashboard from a supported phone, tablet, or computer.

02

Getting Started

Start using the system in a few simple steps.

1

Make sure the system has power

Check that the main equipment is switched on and receiving power.

2

Connect your phone or computer

Use a device that can access the internet.

3

Open the INNOVATEX dashboard

Open the dashboard address provided to you during installation.

4

Check the status

Look for the connection or system status before using the controls.

Ready? If the dashboard shows that the system is connected, you can begin checking your property.

06

Alarm

The alarm is used to warn you about important events.

When an alarm is triggered, check the dashboard message first. If it is safe to do so, check the camera and the monitored area.

Important: an alarm is a warning from the system. Always use your own judgment and follow your local safety procedures.

When an alarm appears

1

Read the message

Find out what caused the warning.

2

Check the camera

Use the camera when available to understand the situation.

3

Stay safe

Do not enter a dangerous area just to investigate an alert.

🌙

Turn OFF

08

Alerts & What They Mean

Use this quick guide when a message appears.

SYSTEM NORMALThe system is working normally and no important warning is active.
MOTION DETECTEDMovement has been detected in the monitored area. Check the camera if needed.
INTRUSION ALERTThe system has detected activity that may require your attention.
FIRE ALERTThe system has detected a possible fire-related condition. Follow your safety procedure immediately.
CONNECTION PROBLEMThe dashboard may not be receiving new information. Check the connection and system power.

Wi-Fi & Connection

A working internet connection helps the dashboard receive new information.

If the system is connected

You can normally use the dashboard, view available information, and receive system updates.

If you changed your Wi-Fi

1

Check the new Wi-Fi

Make sure the new network is working normally on your phone or computer.

2

Reconnect the system

If your installation requires the system to be given the new Wi-Fi details, follow the setup instructions provided by your installer.

3

Check the dashboard

Wait a short time and confirm that new readings are appearing again.

Need help? If you are unsure how to reconnect the installed equipment, contact the person who installed or maintains your INNOVATEX system.

11

Troubleshooting

Try these simple checks before asking for help.

ProblemWhat to check
Dashboard does not openCheck your internet connection and try again.
Data is not changingCheck whether the system is connected and has power.
Camera is not showingWait a moment, refresh the dashboard, and check the system connection.
Motion alert appearsCheck the camera and the monitored area if it is safe.
Lights do not respondCheck the system connection and try the control again.
Connection problem remainsRestart only the equipment you were instructed to restart, then contact support if needed.
Do not open or repair electrical equipment yourself. Contact a qualified person if physical repair is required.
12

Safety

A few simple rules help keep you and the system safe.

Keep the equipment dry unless it is designed for outdoor use.
Do not remove covers or open electrical equipment.
Keep cables away from water, heat, and damage.
Do not rely only on the system during an emergency.
Treat fire and security alerts seriously.
Keep login information private.
